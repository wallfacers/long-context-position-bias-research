Frozen on 2026-08-28 before confirmatory results were available. This map defines which artifacts are allowed to support each paper claim. A result that does not pass the listed gate may be discussed as a diagnostic, but cannot become a headline conclusion.

Amendment frozen 2026-08-29, still before any strict block-96 Qwen or Mistral outcomes: C8 adds a deterministic, descriptive failure taxonomy. It does not add an inferential contrast or change C1--C7.

Procedural amendment on 2026-08-29 after the Qwen NoLiMa stage but before full queue closure: C9 adds compute/cost disclosure only. It cannot change a model-performance claim or select a treatment.

Implementation-correction amendment on 2026-08-29 after Qwen NoLiMa and partial LongBench were visible, but before rule confirmation or any Mistral training: exact reconstruction of the fixed-100 sampler showed that the consumed subsets were identity- and token-matched but not block-complete or exactly position-balanced. Those runs are diagnostic only. C1--C7 primary evidence now requires the 96-row strict block-complete rerun. This correction was triggered by implementation lineage rather than scores, but it is post-hoc for Qwen and must be labeled as such; Mistral remains prospective under the corrected protocol.

## Lineage gates shared by every claim

1. Model generation metadata must bind the pinned revision, full model-manifest hash, stat-revalidated integrity attestation, tokenizer fingerprint, selected chat protocol, data hash, selected sample IDs, adapter hash and decoding parameters. The exploratory Qwen rule process predates the prospective gate and is accepted only with its explicitly post-hoc `generation-lineage-attestation.json`; it never enters a strict-primary seed mean.
2. `prompt-length-audit.json` must prove every rendered prompt plus its frozen output cap fits the declared model window. The exploratory Qwen rule audit is explicitly labeled as a post-hoc CPU re-render; all strict-primary audits must run prospectively before model allocation. Silent truncation invalidates the suite.
3. Every primary block-96 training condition must have checkpoint-96, `CANARY_COMPLETE.json`, a complete step 1–96 metric trace, data/model hashes and publication training curves. The realized-subset audit hashes each pretokenization manifest and Arrow artifact and records the normalized chat protocol; legacy missing/null is accepted only as Qwen's native protocol, while the explicit Mistral merge protocol must remain exact. Historical fixed-100 traces remain separately labeled.
4. The six primary treatment cells within a seed must pass both the full-data matched audit and realized-subset audit: 24 identical fact identities × four replicas, p000/p025/p050/p100 each 24, independent one position per fact, paired four positions per fact, and zero pairing input-token gap.
5. Paper-facing primary numbers may be written only by `generate_paper_results.py` from `primary_training_seed_summary=true` seed-level JSON. The aggregate records each family's eligible statuses: Qwen `corrective`, Mistral `confirmatory`; exploratory fixed-100 analyses are separately hashed but excluded from primary means. The generator emits all six cells and frozen factorial contrasts and deliberately has no cross-condition “best score” macro. Hand-copied or post-hoc best-cell headline numbers are forbidden.
6. Figures must ship with their source-analysis SHA-256 and machine-readable table alternative. The final sanitized release and arXiv source audits must both pass.
7. `results/full_paper_evidence_manifest.json` must revalidate every suite package against its adjacent SHA-256 record and directly hash the cross-family analyses, regression/mechanism reports, generated paper numbers, main figure, table alternative, alt text and final compute-accounting report. It also cross-binds the paper-results manifest to every audited source and generated TeX hash, and the figure manifest to the audited NoLiMa analysis plus PDF/SVG/PNG/CSV/alt-text hashes; separately valid but mutually inconsistent files fail closed.
8. Every strict controlled, NoLiMa, and LongBench suite completion must reference a validated failure-catalog manifest. The primary final audit receives an explicit frozen list of exactly 18 strict block-96 catalogs (Qwen 3 seeds × 3 suites plus Mistral 3 seeds × 3 suites), recomputes every source JSONL row count/hash, and ignores unrelated historical catalogs by construction; all published identifiers are one-way SHA-256 fingerprints. Historical fixed-100 catalogs remain separately labeled diagnostics and cannot silently enter the primary count.

## Interpretation order

Interpret C1–C3 first on the controlled suite, then require C4 or C5 before using the word “transfer.” Use C6 to localize the failure mode, C7 to bound regression, and C8 only to illustrate already reported aggregate patterns. If the controlled suite saturates while C4/C5 show no stable effect, the supported conclusion is distribution-specific repair with no detectable robust-transfer advantage—not universal removal of lost-in-the-middle.
